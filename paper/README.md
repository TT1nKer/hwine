# Historical experiment artifacts

The paper, figures and checked-in result CSVs predate the reliable integer RTL
boundary. They are retained as historical research artifacts. Old claims about
passthrough atoms, e-class "proof", exact DSP prediction and pipeline retiming
are not guarantees of the current implementation. In particular, historical
Vivado numbers do not validate the revised pipeline scheduler.

Use [the current contract](../docs/RELIABILITY.md) and
[the validation record](../docs/VALIDATION.md) for reproducible correctness and
measurement claims. No paper figures or Vivado results were regenerated in this
local reliability pass.
